# I1: screenshots that show changes at a glance

`deploy/shots.sh` (pages from `deploy/shots.py`), documented in `docs/nautilus.md` ("Seeing what a
change did").

- `deploy/shots.sh run NAME [NAME=VALUE...]` renders every place of `dive3d::places::all()` (the
  legs, the other visit's seabed, the cave tunnel, swipes and submarine encounters: 87 places)
  into `target/shots/NAME/` with `index.html`, a contact sheet per sea. New legs are picked up
  because the places come from the voyage's leg list. Two runs of one build are byte-identical
  (checked: 0 changed places).
- `deploy/shots.sh compare A B` writes `target/shots/compare-A-B.html`: per place before | after |
  difference x16, the share of pixels that moved by more than 2/255, mean and max change, most
  changed first, a per-sea summary, identical places folded away; the table also goes to stdout.
- `PI=<user>@<pi> deploy/shots.sh pi NAME` does the same on the Pi through the test binary (GL),
  kiosk frozen under the benchmark lock; tried on the coral places.
- The `screenshots` test now honours `DIVE3D_SKIP` and `DIVE3D_SKIP_EFFECTS`, so a feature's
  look can be shot with it left out.
- Example (coral, Mac, fish left out): `deploy/shots.sh run a DIVE3D_SHOTS=coral`, `... run c
  DIVE3D_SHOTS=coral DIVE3D_SKIP_EFFECTS=1`, `... compare a c` listed 4 of 4 changed, 0.98% of
  pixels at most.

Left: the names of places carry the sea, so a reordered voyage shows as places added and removed
(the page lists them) rather than as changes. `previews` still has its own 14-name list.
