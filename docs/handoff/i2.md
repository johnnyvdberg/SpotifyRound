# I2: what every part and effect costs

`dive3d::feature_costs::features` (`src/dive3d/feature_costs.rs`), `deploy/bench-pi.sh features
TAG`, the table and how to read it in `docs/feature-costs.md`.

- Every pass in `PASSES`, every model group and every sprite kind, at the places where it is in
  view: 84 places (14 seas x 3 places x 2 visits), 6 paired frames each, 4 minutes on the Pi.
- Groups are taken from `effect::MODEL_GROUPS` and `SPRITE_GROUPS`; a new pass needs a row in
  `PASSES` (and its bit in `dive3d::part`).
- First table (GL): frame 8.50 ms; opaque models 1.8 median; open water 1.3; terrain 1.1;
  sargassum 1.7 where present; coral 1.5; ruins 1.4; trees 1.2; the vents' glow sprites 2.6; the
  cave tunnel 2.0 in the cave.
- `bench-pi.sh run` now cleans the Pi's `/tmp` copy of the binary and the output directory.

Left: the 0.05 ms noise floor hides cheap rows; `DIVE3D_FEATURE_FRAMES` can be raised for them.
