# Photographed surfaces

Materials from ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), released under CC0 1.0
Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), fetched at
1K and baked by `deploy/surfaces.py` from `surfaces.tsv` into 512-texel
layers: colour (or its tone) in RGB, the displacement map as height in
alpha. `src/dive3d/textures.rs` reads them as the relief layers of the dive.
